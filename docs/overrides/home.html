See it in action

From quick analyses to end-to-end workflows, see what you can build. Every chart here was drawn by OpenRetailScience with its default styling.

workflow.py 
from openretailscience.segmentation.hml import HMLSegmentation
from openretailscience.segmentation.segstats import SegTransactionStats

segments = HMLSegmentation(transactions)
joined = transactions.left_join(segments.table, "customer_id")
stats = SegTransactionStats(joined, grouping_sets=[("segment_name",)])
df = stats.df.set_index("segment_name")
share = df.spend / df.spend.sum()
print(f"Your top 20% of customers drive {share['Heavy']:.0%} of spend")
